## 1. Architecture

**Shape today.** One asyncio process (`hub.py:255-299`) hosts: whisper + Kokoro warm-up, the aiohttp site (display, `/tts`, `/api/event`), the Alexa puller task, the weather task, the BOX-3 reconnect loop, the voice `Session`, and the `Recorder`. Module-level globals wire them together (`disp`, `REC`, `route`, `tts`, `transcribe` at `hub.py:142-147`). For a single-home hub on one Mac mini this is a reasonable shape — the bottleneck is the GIL-free MLX work, which already runs via `asyncio.to_thread`, and nothing here needs a second process. The findings are about *isolation inside* the process and about what happens on the way up and down.

| # | Sev | Finding | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **M** | **Warm-up blocks everything.** `await asyncio.to_thread(warmup)` runs before the web site, the Alexa puller and the BOX-3 loop start. For 15–45 s after every restart/power cut the frame shows "Offline", Alexa jobs queue up (and may age toward the 10-min "missed" limit if restarts repeat), and the BOX-3 has nothing to connect to. Start the site and the puller first, warm up in the background, and have the frame show "Warming up…" (a `toast`/status is enough). | `hub.py:257-263` |

## 2. Correctness and robustness

| # | Sev | Finding | Where |

| C1 | **M** | **VAD cuts on mid-sentence pauses ≥ ~0.5 s.** With synthetic speech, a 0.5 s pause ("add milk … and eggs") ended the utterance at the pause (`end=1.92 s`, `last_voice=1.25`); 0.4 s (the case `test_session.py` builds) survives. `TRAIL_SIL_S = 0.65` is measured from the last chunk above `max(3×floor, 15 % of peak)`, and the natural decay of a word eats ~0.15 s of that. ### 4.2 How ready is it to grow into a dashboard?

Not very, in its current shape — but the fix is a reorganisation, not a rewrite:

- `render()` (`display.html:114-163`) is one ~50-line `if/else` chain of long single-line template strings. The idle screen is one branch (`display.html:121-127`) that hard-codes the clock column, the weather line, the Record button and a single "Shopping" panel. Adding a second list, a status strip or a "next" tile means editing a 600-character string literal.
- The model `M` (`display.html:90`) is flat and the `snapshot`/`weather` handlers mutate it ad hoc; there is no notion of "a tile's data changed, re-render idle if visible" beyond the special case for `weather` (`display.html:182`).
- On the hub side `Display` already caches `weather`, `media`, `recipe`, `step`, `last_reply` and merges `snapshot_extra` (`display.py:31-38, 68-75`). That is 80 % of a dashboard model; it just has no generic way for a producer (lists, Alexa status, BOX-3 link, timers, HA bridge later) to publish a tile.

**Recommended structure (keep one static file, no build step):**

1. **DISPLAY.md §6 first:** add one generic event and one snapshot field, then everything dashboard-ish flows through them without inventing a type per tile:
   - `panel.update {panel:"<id>", data:{...}}` — replaces that panel's data; the page re-renders idle only if it is showing.
   - `snapshot.panels: {<id>: data}` — so a reconnect restores all tiles.
   - Register the v1 panel ids in §6: `status` (`{box3:bool, alexa:"running|disabled|auth_error|backoff", stt:"xai|whisper", last_heard:{text,ts,source}}`), `lists` (already in snapshot — keep `list.updated` as the typed event because it has behaviour: highlight, toast, foreground), `weather` (fold the existing `weather` event into `panel.update{panel:"weather"}` or keep both; pick one and document it), `next` (empty in v1; timers/calendar later).
   Keep typed events for anything with *behaviour* (`list.updated`, `reply`, `recipe*`, `media.state`, `error`) and use `panel.update` for *display-only* state. That is the line between "dashboard tile" and "foreground event".
2. **`display.py`:** split `Display` into a small `DisplayState` (the cached model incl. `panels`, pure, unit-testable) and the transport (`emit`, `_send_all`, routes).
